Insyr Learning Privacy Policy

Insyr Learning is a CUET and NCET exam preparation app.

Data we collect

  • Name, email address, mobile number and academic stream, provided at registration
  • Optional profile photo
  • Mock test attempts and scores, and feedback you submit
  • Crash and diagnostic data collected by Firebase Crashlytics

How we use it

To provide your account, stream-specific study content, progress tracking, administrator approval of accounts, and support. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, App Check, Crashlytics).

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us in readable form.

Deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Profile > Delete Account, which removes your account, test attempts and feedback. You can also request deletion at the deletion page or by emailing privacy@insyrlearning.com.
