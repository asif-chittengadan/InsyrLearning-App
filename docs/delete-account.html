Delete your Insyr Learning account

In the app

Open Profile, tap Delete Account and confirm. Your account, test attempts and feedback are permanently deleted.

Without the app

Email privacy@insyrlearning.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and associated data (profile, test attempts, feedback, profile photo) after verifying the request.

Crash diagnostics held by Firebase Crashlytics are anonymous and expire automatically.
